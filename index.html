Telegram, Discord, Slack & WhatsApp

  1. Telegram & WhatsApp: Every `.webp` and `.png` file is pre-sized to `512×512` (`<100KB`). Tap "Direct Send" to share straight into a chat, then tap the sent sticker and choose "Add to Favorites".

Install PeelOff on Your Phone Home Screen (PWA)
